### TFLOPS (Tera Floating Point Operations Per Second)
TFLOPS stands for “tera floating point operations per second,” which is a measure of how many trillion floating‑point math operations a processor (like a GPU) can perform every second.

**Floating‑point math operations** are arithmetic calculations performed on real numbers (numbers with decimals) using a computer’s floating‑point unit.
​
These operations include addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and more complex functions (like square root or trigonometric functions) on values such as 3.14159 or 0.00042 rather than just integers.
