### Artificial Intelligence (AI)
AI is the broad idea of machines simulating human intelligence and decision making.
​Humans: Gather information → decide what to do.
AI: Systems do the same—perceive inputs, reason, and take actions (e.g., classify, recommend, control).

### Machine Learning (ML)
ML is a subset of AI where machines learn from data without being explicitly programmed for every rule.
​You give the system examples (scenarios, labeled data).
The system learns patterns and then makes predictions/decisions on new data.

### Deep Learning (DL)
DL is a subset of ML that uses artificial neural networks (ANNs) inspired by the human brain.
​Data passes through layers of artificial “neurons” (input layer → hidden layers → output layer).
These layers learn increasingly complex features (edges → shapes → objects in vision, for example).
​This is how machines “process data in a way inspired by the human brain.”
