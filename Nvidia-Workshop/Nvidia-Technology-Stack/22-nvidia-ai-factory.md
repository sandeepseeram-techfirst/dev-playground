### Nvidia AI Factory
An NVIDIA AI Factory is an AI‑focused data center concept that takes data as raw material and, using NVIDIA’s full hardware–software stack, produces trained/finetuned models and inference outputs as the “finished products.” 

Conceptually, an AI Factory is an AI‑optimized data center that NVIDIA or partners can build and operate, and customers subscribe to or deploy. It is described as specialized computing infrastructure designed to create value from data by managing the AI lifecycle.

**Deployment Options**
​
* On‑premises (your own data center).

* Cloud.

* Hybrid (mix of both).

**It is not yet a simple, universally available product you can immediately purchase and use; rather, it is a direction and set of reference designs for future AI‑centric data centers that can be deployed on‑prem, in cloud, or in hybrid environments.**
