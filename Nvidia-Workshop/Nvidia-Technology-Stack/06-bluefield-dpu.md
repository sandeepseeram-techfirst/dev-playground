### Nvidia Bluefield DPU
BlueField DPUs (also called BlueField SuperNICs) are smart NICs that combine networking, compute, and acceleration to offload infrastructure tasks (network, storage, security) from your CPUs/GPUs.

#### What BlueField DPUs do? 
* Combine networking + compute + acceleration on the NIC itself.
​* Offload tasks like packet processing, load balancing, encryption, encapsulation, and storage I/O from host CPUs/GPUs.
​* Free CPUs/GPUs to focus on application and AI computation, not low‑level infrastructure work.
​* They are programmed and managed using the DOCA (Data Center Infrastructure on a Chip Architecture) software framework.

#### Ecosystem and variants

BlueField DPUs come in various options for:
1. Hyperscale data centers.
2. HPC.
3. AI and cloud computing.
4. Storage acceleration.

#### Design rule of thumb:
Use BlueField DPU when you want a programmable, offload‑capable NIC to handle infrastructure tasks (network, storage, security) so that your main compute (CPU/GPU) can focus on core workloads and AI.
