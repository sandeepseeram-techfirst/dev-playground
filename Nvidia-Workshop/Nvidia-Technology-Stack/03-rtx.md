### RTX 
NVIDIA RTX is a common GPU architecture platform reused across gaming, professional workstations, and some data center GPUs. RTX GPUs include three core hardware engine types: **RT cores**, **tensor cores**, and **CUDA cores**. 

RTX is important for graphics and visualization workloads, but not the primary choice for large‑scale AI training, HPC, or edge/embedded deployments.

#### RTX architecture and variants
**RTX platform**

RTX is a technology/architecture that adds ray tracing (RT cores), tensor cores for AI/math, and CUDA cores for general parallel compute inside one GPU.
​
The same base architecture is tuned into different product lines:

1. Gaming GPUs (e.g., RTX 40‑series desktop cards).

2. Professional workstation GPUs. 

3. Some data center RTX GPUs (e.g., L40 class) used for visualization and virtual workstations.
