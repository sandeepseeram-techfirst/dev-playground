### Nvidia AI Enterprise
NVIDIA AI Enterprise is a complete software suite (“OS for enterprise AI”) that bundles NVIDIA’s AI infrastructure, application software, and management tools so enterprises can build, run, and manage AI safely, efficiently, and at scale across cloud, data center, edge, and workstations. 

#### What NVIDIA AI Enterprise is? 
It is a suite of NVIDIA software, not a single product: a curated, licensed bundle of everything needed to operationalize AI on NVIDIA GPUs.

**Goal:** give companies all tools in one place to build, deploy, and operate AI workloads on NVIDIA hardware safely and efficiently at scale.
​
NVIDIA describes it as the “operating system for enterprise AI” because it sits above raw hardware and organizes drivers, infrastructure software, application services, and management.
