### Nvidia AI Stack 

**Physical layer** – GPUs, systems, interconnects.

**Acceleration & data communication** – NVLink, NVSwitch, PCIe, RDMA, NCCL.

**Operating system & virtualization** – OS, drivers, container runtime, virtualization.
​
**Core libraries** – CUDA and related libraries for GPU programming.
​
**Monitoring & management** – NVIDIA‑SMI, DCGM, Base Command Manager.
​
**Vertical solutions** – domain‑specific stacks like Clara, Merlin, NIM.
