### MLOps
ML Ops is about running ML like a disciplined, well‑managed kitchen: standardizing, automating, and monitoring everything so models give consistent, reliable results over time.

#### With ML Ops

* Every model is documented and versioned.
​
* Training data is tracked and stored properly, and updated when needed.
​
* Training and deployment steps are automated and repeatable via pipelines.
​
* Continuous monitoring checks model accuracy and behavior in production.
​
**Result: consistent, trustworthy predictions aligned with business expectations.**
