### Nvidia Certified Associate - Generative AI & LLM's 

The **NVIDIA-Certified Associate:** Generative AI and LLMs (NCA-GENL) is an entry-level certification validating foundational skills in developing, integrating, and maintaining AI-driven applications using generative AI and large language models with NVIDIA solutions.

The examination test, and will focus on developing, integrating, and maintaining the AI application using Nvidia solutions. 

**Core machine learning and AI knowledge:** foundational concepts that generative AI and LLMs build upon.
​
**Software development:** how to work with AI and LLMs in the context of real applications.
​
**Data analysis:** preparing, understanding, and working with data that powers AI models.
​
**Data visualization:** techniques for presenting and interpreting AI‑related data effectively.
