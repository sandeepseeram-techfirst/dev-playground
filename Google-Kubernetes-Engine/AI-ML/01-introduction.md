### AI/ML on GKE 

1. Serving Large Language Models (LLMs) efficiently on Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) requires understanding various architectural patterns and optimization strategies. 

Deploying LLMs in production isn't just about running a model—it's about balancing performance, cost, scalability, and reliability. Different models have different requirements: a 2B parameter model can run on a single GPU, while a 70B parameter model needs multiple GPUs working together.
