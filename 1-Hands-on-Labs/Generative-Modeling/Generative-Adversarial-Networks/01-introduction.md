## Generative Adversarial Networks

Generative adversarial networks, **GANs**, have become the basis for many applications of generative modeling.

Generative modeling is the basis for applications of:

1. Text generation—Fake News
Facial swapping—Deep Fakes
Pose translation
Static image animation
Medical imaging analysis
Generating fake art
Feature image analysis
Restoring images and video
GANs provide a foundation for building generative modeling on many varied applications.
