## Cloud-Native AI Hands-on Lab 
- Audience: Cloud / Platform / SRE / DevOps engineers moving into AI/ML on Kubernetes
- Level: Beginner → Intermediate
- Cluster: 3‑node Kubernetes (CPU only)

#### By the end of this lab, you will have:

- A working end‑to‑end AI/ML platform on Kubernetes

- Hands‑on exposure to all major CNAI domains

- Built a real ML system: data ingestion → training → experiment tracking → model registry → serving → monitoring → pipelines → governance

- Intermediate‑level practical skill in Cloud‑Native AI

- You will build a realistic ML platform for an “Intelligent Log Analyzer” that predicts anomaly severity from log features.
