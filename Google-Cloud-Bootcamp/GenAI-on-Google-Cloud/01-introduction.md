# Generative AI on Google Cloud Platform

In today’s AI-driven world, success with Generative AI (GenAI) depends not just on creating powerful models, but on deploying them effectively in production.

### 🚀 Key Objectives for Bootcamp

*Bridge the Prototype-to-Production Gap*
Learn frameworks and workflows that help move 75% of stalled GenAI projects into production successfully.

*Operate at Enterprise Scale*
Use Google Cloud’s Vertex AI and related tools to handle model training, deployment, monitoring, and integration seamlessly.

*Deliver Measurable Business Value*
Go beyond experimentation — focus on real-world performance, ROI, and operational excellence.
