# Generative AI on Google Cloud Platform

In today’s AI-driven world, success with Generative AI (GenAI) depends not just on creating powerful models, but on deploying them effectively in production.

### 🚀 Key Objectives for Bootcamp

*Bridge the Prototype-to-Production Gap*
Learn frameworks and workflows that help move 75% of stalled GenAI projects into production successfully.

*Operate at Enterprise Scale*
Use Google Cloud’s Vertex AI and related tools to handle model training, deployment, monitoring, and integration seamlessly.

*Deliver Measurable Business Value*
Go beyond experimentation — focus on real-world performance, ROI, and operational excellence.


### 🏗️ Core Focus Areas

1. LLM Operations (LLMOps)

- Understand data pipelines, evaluation systems, and responsible AI practices.

- Build workflows that ensure compliance, observability, and scalability.

2. Enterprise AI Architecture

- Design multi-agent systems and tool-integrated GenAI platforms.

- Support multimodal data (text, images, video) in robust and production-ready environments.

3. Google Cloud Vertex AI

Hands-on examples for:

- Training and fine-tuning LLMs

- Deploying scalable GenAI endpoints

- Monitoring model health and performance

- Integrating with enterprise tools and data systems

4. Optimization and Cost Management

- Apply proven strategies to balance performance with cost efficiency.

- Use Vertex AI optimization tools and model monitoring to reduce operational overhead.
