# Gen AI & Agentic AI Applications 

GenAI and Agentic AI serve distinct purposes and operate in fundamentally different ways. Let’s look at how each works in four common applications. 

 
The first application is customer support. GenAI works well as a chatbot, answering specific questions relating to a topic that it has been trained on. Agentic AI acts more as a personalized customer service agent. It draws on past interactions across multiple users and channels to offer suggestions for usage, support, and fixes. It can also make offers for additional services. 
 
The next application is software development. GenAI acts as a coding assistant when paired with a programmer. It executes simple tasks on behalf of developers, such as coding a simple Java script. The power of Agentic is to mimic the entire software workflow, not just coding.  For example, one agent might do requirements gathering, another high-level architecture, another coding, and another testing. This can be followed by deployment. A different agent executes ongoing monitoring and real-time maintenance and updates.  

Agentic AI agents can also complete complex programming exercises and orchestrate specialized agents, coding across multiple frameworks and languages. This might include specialized coding agents for user experience, security, and cloud integration. There are also expert agents for software architecture, database, and testing.  
 
Another application is research and analysis. GenAI research assistants provide answers and source material for specific requests. An example of this is how Perplexity AI provides instant answers on a wide variety of topics. OpenAI's DeepResearch is a prime example of Agentic AI because it autonomously performs complex, multi-step tasks with minimal human intervention. It actively searches the internet for relevant information, synthesizes and interprets data, generates visualizations, and proposes actionable insights—mirroring the cognitive workflow of a human researcher. This demonstrates how Agentic AI can independently pursue goals, adapt to new information, and make informed decisions. 
 
The final application is cybersecurity. GenAI works as a security operations assistant. It reviews current logs, summarizes incidents, and recommends actions. While helpful, these would fall into the simple task management category. On the other hand, Agentic AI functions as a cyber defender of systems. It reviews the security posture and current health of the systems. This cyber defender proactively adjusts the security posture and responds to specific cyber attacks. It can even loop in specific experts when needed.
