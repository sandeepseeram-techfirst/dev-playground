# Fundamentals of Agentic AI 

### The Concept of Agency 
Agency is defined as “the capacity, condition, or state of acting or exerting power,” as well as “a means through which power is exerted or an end is achieved.”  In essence, agency refers to the ability to take action to bring about a desired outcome. Achieving agency requires key capabilities such as planning, action, memory, and reflection—all of which are fundamental to modern AI agents.
