# Agentic AI - Use Cases 

Agentic AI transforms nearly every industry, from retail to healthcare to government. 

How is this achieved? By driving efficiency and automation in a wide range of applications. 

As Agentic AI evolves, its ability to streamline workflows and improve decision-making makes it an integral part of industry innovation. 

Let’s take a closer look at two industries where Agentic AI is making a significant impact: healthcare and financial services. 

In healthcare, intelligent AI agents continuously monitor sensor data to detect even subtle changes in a patient’s condition. When a change is identified, these agents analyze the situation and determine the necessary adjustments to the patient’s care plan. More importantly, they assess which changes are critical enough to require a doctor’s intervention and immediately alert medical professionals. This ability to triage alerts and provide real-time insights enhances patient care, reduces response times, and optimizes hospital resources.    

How does this play out in reality? You receive an email from a clinic asking you to log in to its Agentic AI agent. That agent verifies clerical details such as home address, consent-to-treat forms, insurance, etc. It ensures it has the most up-to-date family and patient medical history. It then asks a series of questions to obtain additional information on your condition. By cross-referencing your symptoms with your family and personal medical history, it formulates a diagnosis and recommends a course of treatment. All of this would be done prior to visiting your physician or is done instead of a visit. This is how Agentic AI makes the healthcare process more effective and efficient. 

In financial services, Agentic AI enables real-time market analysis. This allows it to identify new investment opportunities that align with a client’s portfolio. In addition to investment portfolio development, it enables clients to develop comprehensive and personalized financial plans. That includes budgeting, tax minimization strategies, insurance need integration, retirement drawdown planning, and more.  

For firms engaged in high-frequency trading, Agentic AI detects market inefficiencies and executes trades within nanoseconds, capitalizing on fleeting opportunities before they disappear. This level of speed and precision allows financial institutions to optimize trading strategies and maximize returns in a highly dynamic market.   

As these examples illustrate, Agentic AI doesn’t just automate tasks; it enables more intelligent, data-driven decision-making across industries.
